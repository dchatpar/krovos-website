About Krovos

Building the future of enterprise technology, one transformation at a time.

Our Story

From Vision to Global Enterprise

Krovos was founded in 2014 with a singular belief: enterprise technology should empower, not complicate. What started as a small team of engineers in San Francisco has grown into a global enterprise serving 500+ clients across 40+ countries.

Today, we're proud to be the strategic technology partner of choice for Fortune 500 companies, growth-stage startups, and everything in between. Our integrated approach combines deep technical expertise with strategic business understanding.

Our Mission

To help enterprises innovate faster, scale smarter, and compete in an increasingly complex world.

We believe technology should simplify, not complicate. Every solution we deliver is designed to create measurable impact—reducing costs, increasing revenue, and building sustainable competitive advantages for our clients.
